What is Body Mass Index (BMI)?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a widely accepted measurement used to estimate whether an individual has a healthy body weight relative to their height. By entering your weight in kilograms and height in centimeters, our tool categorizes your result into underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese ranges.

Standard BMI Categories:

  • Underweight: Less than 18.5
  • Normal Weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0 and above
