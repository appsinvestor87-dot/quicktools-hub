How to Keep Your Online Accounts Secure

In today's digital world, cyber threats are common, and weak passwords are the primary reason accounts get hacked. Using a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols makes your password nearly impossible to guess or crack.

Tips for Password Safety:

  • Never use the same password across multiple websites.
  • Aim for a password length of at least 12 to 16 characters.
  • Avoid using personal information like birthdays or simple names.
